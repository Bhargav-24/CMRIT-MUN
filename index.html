A chamber built for bold ideas

CMRIT MUN is the flagship Model United Nations conference of CMR Institute of Technology, Hyderabad. Over several days, students from colleges across Telangana and beyond step into the shoes of ambassadors, argue world affairs, and draft resolutions that actually have to survive a vote.

Whether this is your first placard or your fiftieth, you will leave sharper, braver and better connected.

    Questions, answered

    Who can attend?

    Students from any school or college. Delegations and individual delegates are both welcome.

    Do I need prior experience?

    No. Beginner friendly committees and pre-conference training are planned for first timers.

    Where is the conference?

    On the CMR Institute of Technology campus, Kandlakoya, Medchal, Hyderabad, Telangana.

    When do registrations open?

    Soon. Follow @cmritmun on Instagram or leave your details above.
